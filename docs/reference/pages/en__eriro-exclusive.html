Tranquility meets exclusivity

The mountain is calling. Sometimes loudly, sometimes quietly, and everything else falls silent. The tranquillity on the alp is powerful and pristine. It creates room for the essential. Exclusivity is not an extra here - it is the essence. A place for encounters that don't need a stage. For moments that cannot be captured and yet remain forever. Eriro becomes a personal retreat, welcoming guests exclusively for special moments in complete privacy and stillness with family, friends or colleagues. 

Nine suites, surrounded by stone and wood, overlooking the untouched wilderness of the mountains. Nothing is loud here, nothing is distracting. No must, only be. With the people who matter. 

We are happy to create a bespoke experience on request hide@eriro.at.

Alpine Hide

  • 1,800 square metres of luxury space for a maximum of 22 - 24 guests.
  • 9 suites from 49 to 163 square metres with private terrace or loggia with direct access to the alpine meadows and views of Zugspitze
  • Rooftop suite with 2 loggias and private sauna
  • High-quality suite amenities such as Dyson hairdryer, record player, jukebox, curated book selection
  • Guests under the age of 16 are also welcome when booking the entire space
