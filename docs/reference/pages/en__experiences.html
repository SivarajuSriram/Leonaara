Memories that anchor in the heart

In the remote corners of the Tyrolean Alps, we become one with nature. A humbleness towards the magnificent mountains shapes all aspects of life. The seasons and their shifts in weather govern each day. Gracious and gentle, archaic and rugged, the mountain calls upon us to be alert and aware with all our senses – it sharpens the eye for the essentials. Experiences and traditions become lasting memories. Exploration, perception and connecting with yourself opens wide the dimension of being.
